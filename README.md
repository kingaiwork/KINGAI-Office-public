# KINGAI Office

## English

**A modern productivity suite for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF workflows and intelligent assistance.**

KINGAI Office is the productivity product family of the KING AI ecosystem. It is being built around a simple idea: everyday office work should be easier to create, edit, understand and move between devices without forcing users into one vendor's ecosystem.

### Product direction

- Writer for rich documents and everyday writing;
- Sheets for structured data, calculations and analysis;
- Slides for presentations and storytelling;
- PDF viewing and document workflows;
- compatibility with widely used office-document formats;
- local-first and privacy-aware intelligent assistance;
- desktop, web/PWA and future broader device experiences;
- a long-term path toward collaborative, AI-assisted productivity.

### Why users may want it

Office software is used every day, but much of it still feels heavier than it needs to be. KINGAI Office aims to make the common tasks fast and familiar while leaving room for intelligent help where it genuinely saves time.
